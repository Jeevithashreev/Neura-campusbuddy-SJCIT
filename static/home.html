How it works

Three steps, from the camera to the savings.
1DetectA camera counts the people in a room. Only a number is sent, and no faces or video are stored.
2ActIf a room is empty with the lights on, the system warns, then switches the lights off after a short delay.
3SaveThe dashboard shows the energy and rupees saved for each block, and the leaderboard ranks them.
